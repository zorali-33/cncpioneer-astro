CNC Turning and Milling Service

CNC Turn-Mill machining is suitable for a wide range of materials including metals (aluminum, brass, free-machining steels, stainless steel, carbon/alloy steels, titanium, copper alloys, superalloys), engineering plastics, magnesium alloys, and special controlled-expansion alloys.
