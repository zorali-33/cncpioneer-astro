CNC Machining Heat Sinks FAQ

Which CNC Machining Heat Sinks designs can you manufacture?

The product range includes Computer Heat Sink CNC Machining, Power Electronics Heat Sink Machining, LED and Lighting Heat Sink Machining. Custom geometry is reviewed against the approved drawing.

  • Include the mating interfaces, critical datums and expected order quantity.
Which materials can I specify?

Material options include 6063-T5, 6061-T6, 6082-T6 & 7075-T6. The exact grade and condition should be confirmed for your application.

  • Include material certificate, finish and any restricted-substance requirements with your enquiry.
What should I include in the drawing?

Define flow channels, sealing lands and mounting flatness, together with datums, tolerances and required surface condition.

  • Include a 2D drawing and 3D model where available.
  • Identify the dimensions that affect assembly or function.
What finishing and inspection records can be requested?

Finishing options include type ii clear anodize, black anodize (type ii or type iii), type iii hard anodize. Applicability is reviewed against the specified material and geometry.

  • Agree material certificates, dimensional reports and any PPAP or FAIR requirements with the order.
How are pricing and lead time confirmed?

Pricing and delivery are confirmed after reviewing the drawing, material availability, quantity, machining route, finishing and inspection scope.

  • Prototype and repeat-production schedules are quoted for the specific order.
