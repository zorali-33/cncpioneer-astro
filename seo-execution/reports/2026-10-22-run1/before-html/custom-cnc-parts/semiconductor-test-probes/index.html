Semiconductor Test Probes: Product Overview

Semiconductor test probes create temporary electrical contact between ATE equipment and wafers, packaged ICs, sockets or power modules during production test and qualification.

The value of a probe is measured by repeatable contact: stable resistance, accurate tip geometry, consistent force, clean plating and predictable life across many touchdowns or insertion cycles.

  • +
    ±0.002mm shaft toleranceSwiss CNC machining supports fine shaft control, tip angle accuracy and repeatable guide-plate fit for wafer and socket probe applications.
  • +
    Complete probe component rangeVertical wafer pins, cantilever tips, BGA/LGA socket probes, burn-in probes, power probes, RF coaxial probes and probe-card hardware.
  • +
    Tungsten and specialty alloy machiningDedicated processes for tungsten, rhenium-tungsten, BeCu, Paliney, Kovar, Invar, stainless steel and semiconductor-grade polymers.
  • +
    Cost-effective China productionPrototype and production programs can combine competitive machining cost with optical inspection, XRF plating records and clean packaging.
