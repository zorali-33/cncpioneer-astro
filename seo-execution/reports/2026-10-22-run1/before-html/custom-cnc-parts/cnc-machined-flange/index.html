CNC Machined Flanges: Product Overview

Custom flange machining produces non-standard pipe flanges, flange adapters and connection flanges whose bore, pilot register, sealing face, bolt pattern, surface finish and external geometry match a defined equipment interface rather than a catalog part.

The critical control point is the relationship between those features.

CNC Machined Flanges FAQ

Which CNC Machined Flanges designs can you manufacture?

The product range includes CNC Turning Custom Pipe Flanges, Custom Aluminum CNC Machining Flanges, Custom Flange Adapter CNC Programs. Custom geometry is reviewed against the approved drawing.

  • Include the mating interfaces, critical datums and expected order quantity.
Which materials can I specify?

Material options include Aluminum 7075-T6, Aluminum 6061-T6, Steel 42CrMo4. The exact grade and condition should be confirmed for your application.

  • Include material certificate, finish and any restricted-substance requirements with your enquiry.
What should I include in the drawing?

Define threads, bores and sealing surfaces, together with datums, tolerances and required surface condition.

  • Include a 2D drawing and 3D model where available.
  • Identify the dimensions that affect assembly or function.
What finishing and inspection records can be requested?

Finishing options include type iii hard anodize, passivation & electropolish, electroless nickel. Applicability is reviewed against the specified material and geometry.

  • Agree material certificates, dimensional reports and any PPAP or FAIR requirements with the order.
How are pricing and lead time confirmed?

Pricing and delivery are confirmed after reviewing the drawing, material availability, quantity, machining route, finishing and inspection scope.

  • Prototype and repeat-production schedules are quoted for the specific order.
