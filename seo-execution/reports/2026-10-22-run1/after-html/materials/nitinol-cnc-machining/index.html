Nitinol Parts: Material and Drawing Review

Nitinol components require a defined material and functional specification. Specify whether the design relies on superelastic or shape-memory behavior and identify the relevant transformation-temperature and test requirements. Geometry, supplied condition and subsequent processing must be assessed together; a machining quote does not by itself validate device performance.

  • Provide the NiTi material specification, supplier certificate and supplied condition; do not use an unverified grade label.
  • Define the intended function, transformation-temperature requirements and any required functional test.
  • Identify critical geometry, surface condition, edge requirements and allowed post-processing.

FAQs

What should I specify for a Nitinol machining quote?

Include the NiTi material specification and supplied condition, intended function, and any transformation-temperature or functional-test requirements. Mark critical features, permitted finishing and acceptance criteria on your drawing.

How are tolerances and acceptance checks agreed?

Identify critical dimensions, datums and functional surfaces on the drawing. Feasibility, inspection method, material condition and any post-processing allowances are reviewed before acceptance; no single tolerance applies to every part.

CNC Machining Learning Center

What is Swiss Machining?

Swiss machining uses a sliding headstock and guide bushing, unlike standard lathes, to support material while the tool remains stationary.

What is CNC Turning and Milling?

In CNC milling, the tool moves while the material is stationary. In CNC turning, the material spins while the tool moves.
