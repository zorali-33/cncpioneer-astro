CNC Machining Questions

Which CNC machining process fits my part?

Swiss machining is a useful starting point for small bar-stock parts with slender sections or fine features. CNC turning and milling suits parts that combine cylindrical surfaces with milled or drilled features. Send your drawing so the process can be reviewed against material, geometry, quantity and inspection needs.

What tolerances can you quote?

Specify the tolerances, datums and surface finish that the part needs to function. Feasibility is reviewed feature by feature because material, geometry, workholding and measurement access affect the result. The accepted drawing and quotation define the requirements for your order.

Which materials can I request?

Material guides cover metals such as stainless steel, aluminum, brass and titanium, and engineering plastics such as PEEK and POM. State the exact grade and stock condition in your request; availability and suitability must be confirmed for the particular part.

How are quantity and delivery time confirmed?

Include the initial quantity, likely repeat demand, target date and destination. Material availability, setup, machining, finishing and inspection affect cost and timing. Batch size and any expedited schedule are confirmed with the quotation.

Can I request certification and inspection documents?

Yes, include the required standard, report type, material records and traceability scope with your enquiry. Availability and applicability must be confirmed before ordering. If supplier certification is mandatory, request the current certificate and check that its scope meets your project requirements.
