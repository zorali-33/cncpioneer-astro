Brass

Brass is a highly workable and attractive metal alloy prized for its excellent machinability, good corrosion resistance, and distinctive golden appearance. It combines impressive strength with superior electrical and thermal conductivity, making it a go-to material for components that require both functionality and aesthetic appeal. Brass is widely used in plumbing fittings, valves, connectors, musical instruments, decorative hardware, electrical terminals, gears, bushings, marine components, and precision turned parts.

Magnesium

Magnesium sees broad adoption in industries such as automotive, aerospace, electronics, medical devices, sports equipment, and consumer products. Typical uses include gearbox housings, transmission cases, wheel rims, drone frames, laptop chassis, helicopter components, orthopedic implants, surgical tools, bicycle parts, camera bodies, and heat sinks.

FAQs

What is metal CNC machining?

Metal CNC machining is a precise manufacturing process that uses computer-controlled (CNC) machines to remove material from a solid block of metal to create a desired shape. Using 3-axis or 5-axis milling and turning machines, it can produce complex geometries with high accuracy and tight tolerances. This subtractive process is ideal for both prototypes and production parts across many industries.

What types of metal parts can be made with CNC machining?

With CNC metal machining, you can produce almost any component that can be designed in 3D CAD software. This includes complex prototypes, functional end-use parts, single-piece designs with intricate features, and high-volume production runs. Common examples include brackets, housings, shafts, fittings, aerospace components, medical devices, automotive parts, and more — especially parts that benefit from being machined from a single block of metal.

How much does metal CNC machining cost?

The cost of metal CNC machining depends on several factors:

  • Complexity and size of the part
  • Type of metal used (e.g. aluminum, stainless steel, titanium, etc.)
  • Required tolerances and surface finish
  • Quantity (low-volume vs. high-volume)
  • Type of machining needed (milling, turning, 5-axis, etc.) Advanced platforms use machine learning to instantly calculate precise costs from your CAD file based on real historical data. You can usually get an accurate quote within seconds by uploading your design.

What is CNC metal milling and when should I choose it?

CNC metal milling is a machining process where a high-speed rotating cutting tool removes material from a solid metal block or sheet to create the desired shape. The workpiece is mounted on a table that can rotate or tilt, allowing the tool to access multiple angles. Choose milling when:

  • You need complex 3D geometries
  • Parts are machined from a single block of metal
  • You require high precision and excellent surface finish
  • The design includes deep pockets, contours, or detailed features It is one of the most versatile and accurate methods for metal CNC fabrication.
