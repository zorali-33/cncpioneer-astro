FAQ's

What is your typical lead time for CNC machining orders?

Standard lead time is 3–7 days (most parts ship in 3–5 days). For simple parts with available material, we routinely hit 3-day delivery—often faster than anyone else in the industry.

What tolerances can your CNC machines achieve?

Standard tolerance is ±0.01 mm (0.0004″). With advanced 3-/4-/5-axis machines and in-process inspection, we routinely hold ±0.005 mm (0.0002″) or tighter when required.

What types of materials work with your CNC machining services?

Metals: Aluminum (6061, 7075, MIC-6…), Stainless Steel (304, 316, 17-4PH…), Titanium, Brass, Copper, Invar, etc. Plastics: POM (Delrin), PC, ABS, PEEK, PTFE, ULTEM, Nylon, Acrylic, etc. Almost any engineering-grade material—feel free to ask if you have something exotic.

Can you accommodate rush orders or expedited delivery for CNC machining?

Yes—24-hour and 48-hour expedited options are available for most parts. We’ve delivered complex aluminum parts in under 24 hours when needed. Just mention “rush” when quoting.

What certifications or industry standards do you adhere to?

ISO 9001:2015 certified Full material certifications & inspection reports (CMM, RoHS, REACH, CoC) available on request ITAR compliant (for U.S. defense-related projects)

CNC Machining Learning Center

What is Swiss Machining?

Swiss machining uses a sliding headstock and guide bushing, unlike standard lathes, to support material while the tool remains stationary.

What is CNC Turning and Milling？

In CNC milling, the tool moves while the material is stationary. In CNC turning, the material spins while the tool moves.

CNC Machining Materials: A Comprehensive Guide

CNC machining can be applied to a wide variety of materials, including but not limited to metals, plastics, and composites.
