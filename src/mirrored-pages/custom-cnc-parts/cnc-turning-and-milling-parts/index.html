What Is Turn-Mill Compound Machining

What Are CNC Turning and Milling Parts?

CNC Turning and Milling Parts are precision components produced on a turn-mill compound machining center — a single machine that integrates a CNC lathe with live milling spindles, C-axis and Y-axis control. In one clamping, the machine executes turning, milling, drilling, tapping, grooving, and threading together.

Traditional: CNC Lathe → Transfer → Machining Center

  • Part turned on lathe → unclamped → moved → re-fixtured on separate milling center
  • Each re-clamping introduces positioning error — concentricity and true position degrade
  • Longer lead time, higher WIP, more operator touches, higher cost per part

Turn-Mill Compound: 1 Machine · 1 Setup

  • Turning + milling + drilling + threading all in one clamping, one continuous cycle
  • Single datum maintained across all features — concentricity held to ±0.005mm
  • Complex multi-feature parts finished faster, cheaper, and more accurately

Core-Walking (Swiss-type)

Sliding headstock. Workpiece feeds through guide bushing. Best for slender, small-diameter parts (Ø0.5–25mm) with long L/D ratios.

Knife-Walking (Fixed headstock)

Fixed headstock with live tooling turret. Best for larger-diameter, shorter parts (Ø5–150mm) with off-axis milled features and complex geometry.
