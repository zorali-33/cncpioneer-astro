What Are
Crimp Contact Pins?

Crimp contact pins are precision-machined electrical contact components that terminate wire conductors through mechanical crimping — compressing the crimp barrel around the stripped wire conductor to create a gas-tight, low-resistance electrical connection without soldering. They form the male contact element in aerospace wiring harnesses, automotive wire harness systems, industrial control panels, military electronics, and telecommunications infrastructure.

A crimp contact pin consists of three precision zones: the contact pin zone (male pin establishing the electrical contact interface), the transition zone (governing retention in the connector insert), and the crimp barrel zone (accepting the stripped wire and deforming plastically during crimping). Each zone has distinct dimensional requirements governing different aspects of electrical and mechanical performance — all within a single small-diameter component.

Plating & Surface Finishing
for Crimp Contact Pins

Surface plating is a functional requirement for crimp contact pins governing contact resistance at the pin-to-socket interface, corrosion resistance in operating environments, solderability at the wire-to-crimp interface, and mating cycle durability. CNCPioneer's Ra 0.2μm contact surface finish ensures uniform precious metal plating distribution on every contact geometry.

Hard Gold — MIL-G-45204 Class 1

The aerospace and high-reliability industry standard for crimp contact pin mating surfaces. Cobalt-hardened gold (99.7% Au, HV 130–200) applied 0.76μm (30μin) to 2.54μm (100μin) over nickel underplate per MIL-G-45204. Provides stable low contact resistance across temperature extremes and thousands of mating cycles. XRF plating thickness verification every production lot.

Silver Plating — QQ-S-365 / ASTM B700

Preferred plating for high-current power crimp contacts. Silver's superior electrical and thermal conductivity reduces resistive heating at high current densities in aerospace and industrial power distribution applications. Tarnish inhibitor treatment applied for storage and handling protection. Also used on MIL-DTL-5015 power contacts for maximum current-carrying capacity.

Matte Tin — MIL-T-10727 / ASTM B545

The most cost-effective plating for automotive and commercial crimp contact pins. Excellent solderability for wire harness assembly. Matte tin preferred over bright tin to minimize tin whisker risk in automotive electronics. RoHS and ELV compliant — standard for high-volume automotive connector programs. Also available: tin-lead (60/40) for legacy mil-spec and non-RoHS programs.

Palladium-Nickel Plating

Cost-effective hard plating for crimp contact pins requiring better wear resistance than tin but lower cost than gold. Palladium-nickel (80% Pd, 20% Ni) provides HV 400–600 hardness with good oxidation resistance for moderate-reliability connector applications in telecommunications and industrial equipment.

Nickel Underplate — AMS 2403

Applied as a diffusion barrier beneath gold, silver, and palladium on all crimp contact pin mating surfaces to prevent base metal diffusion into the precious metal layer. Electroless nickel for uniform thickness on complex contact geometries; electrolytic nickel for high-speed production. AMS 2403 and MIL-C-26074 compliant.

Selective Plating

Gold or silver on the mating contact zone — tin or nickel on the crimp barrel and body — reduces precious metal consumption by 40–60% versus full-body gold plating while maintaining full contact performance at the critical mating interface. CNCPioneer's crimp contact pins machining produces the shoulder geometry accuracy that selective plating zone boundary control requires for consistent partial-plating results.

Crimp Contact Pins FAQ

Common questions from connector manufacturers, wire harness assemblers, and electrical equipment OEMs about CNCPioneer's crimp contact pins machining capabilities and China crimp contact pins supply programs.

A crimp contact pin terminates wire conductors through mechanical crimping — compressing the crimp barrel around the stripped wire conductor to create a permanent gas-tight electrical connection without heat. A solder cup contact terminates wire conductors through soldering, filling the cup-shaped wire termination zone with molten solder. Crimp termination is preferred in aerospace and automotive applications because it produces a gas-tight, consistent, and repeatable wire-to-contact connection immune to solder joint fatigue, thermal cycling degradation, and flux residue contamination. Crimp contact pins machining must produce precise crimp barrel bore diameter and wall thickness for consistent crimp height after termination — the critical parameter governing crimp contact pull-out force and contact resistance in aerospace and automotive wiring.

MIL-DTL-38999 Series III connector contacts require contact pin diameter tolerances governing mating force compliance with the female socket contact. Size 20 contacts (nominal Ø1.02mm) require contact pin diameter within ±0.004mm of nominal for mating force compliance within the 0.6–2.0 Newton range. Size 16 contacts (nominal Ø1.60mm) require ±0.005mm. Size 8 power contacts (nominal Ø3.18mm) require ±0.006mm. These tolerances are tighter than commercial connector specifications because MIL-DTL-38999 contacts must maintain consistent mating force across multiple cycles at military temperature extremes and after exposure to salt spray, humidity, and vibration. CNCPioneer verifies contact pin diameters by 100% CCD automatic sorting, with CMM dimensional report in the FAIR documentation package.

For aerospace crimp contact pins in MIL-DTL-38999 and MIL-DTL-83513 applications, we recommend 0.76μm (30μin) minimum gold over 1.27μm (50μin) minimum nickel underplate for moderate-reliability applications with mating cycle requirements below 500 cycles. For high-reliability aerospace contacts requiring 500–1,000 mating cycles, 1.27μm (50μin) minimum gold over nickel is recommended. For space and satellite applications requiring maximum contact reliability over 15+ year mission lifetimes, 2.54μm (100μin) gold over nickel provides the margin needed to maintain contact resistance stability across the operational life. All gold plating is cobalt-hardened hard gold per MIL-G-45204 Class 1 over electrolytic nickel, with XRF thickness verification on every production lot.

Crimp barrel bore diameter tolerance of ±0.004mm to ±0.005mm is required for consistent crimp termination results. The crimp barrel bore governs wire insertion fit — the clearance between the stripped wire conductor bundle and the crimp barrel bore before crimping — which affects the degree of crimp deformation and resulting crimp height after termination. Crimp height is the critical crimp quality parameter: too large indicates insufficient conductor compression and low pull-out force; too small indicates over-compression and potential conductor strand damage. Consistent bore diameter within ±0.004mm ensures consistent crimp height when using the same wire gauge and crimping tool die, enabling reliable crimp quality control in wire harness assembly operations.

The crimp barrel zone receives tin plating for wire-to-contact galvanic compatibility and solderability.
