Why Swiss CNC for Electrical
Connector Manufacturing?

Swiss CNC guide-bushing support is well suited to long, slender connector contacts and socket features. It helps maintain diameter, roundness, concentricity and surface finish across prototype and production electrical connector machining programs.

01

Contact Pin Diameter Control

Diameter tolerance to ±0.003mm and roundness to ±0.002mm help stabilize mating force, retention and contact resistance in high-volume connector programs.

02

Socket Bore Concentricity

Socket bore concentricity to the OD helps keep spring force distribution consistent around the mating pin, improving withdrawal force and contact stability.

03

Contact Surface Finish

Ra 0.2µm contact surfaces can be produced for mating zones where plating adhesion, contact resistance and wear behavior depend on a stable finish.

04

Retention and Anti-Backout Geometry

Multi-axis Swiss machining supports retention lances, grooves, shoulders, polarization features and crimp transitions without moving the part through many setups.

05

100% CCD Sorting

Critical contact pin and socket dimensions can be sorted automatically at production speed, supporting special-characteristic control for IATF 16949 connector programs.

06

Turned Component Production

Stable material sourcing, tool-life control, statistical monitoring and lot traceability support repeatable production of turned connector components at program volume.

Component Types

Plating & Surface Finishing for
Electrical Connector Manufacturing

Plating determines contact resistance, corrosion protection, mating durability and solderability. CNCPioneer coordinates connector plating through qualified partners and verifies thickness by XRF when required.

Gold Plating

Hard gold is used for high-reliability signal contacts, aerospace contacts and low-level circuits. Selective gold can reduce precious metal cost on automotive and industrial programs.

Silver Plating

Silver is used for high-current power terminals and EV charging contacts where conductivity and heat control are more important than tarnish sensitivity.

Tin Plating

Tin is widely used for automotive and commercial connector contacts because it is cost-effective, solderable and compatible with RoHS/ELV programs.

Palladium-Nickel Plating

Palladium-nickel is a hard, wear-resistant contact finish for moderate-cycle applications that need better durability than tin at lower cost than gold.

Nickel Underplate

Nickel is commonly used below gold, silver and palladium finishes to prevent base metal diffusion and improve plating durability on contact surfaces.

Rhodium Plating

Rhodium is reserved for demanding, high-cycle contact applications that require a very hard and wear-resistant surface finish.

XRF thickness records, plating certificates and adhesion test results can be included in the shipment package according to the drawing, specification or purchase order.
