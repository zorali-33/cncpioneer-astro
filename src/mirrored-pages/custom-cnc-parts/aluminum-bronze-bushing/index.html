Aluminum Bronze Bushing
Product Portfolio

Plain sleeves, flanges, thrust washers, wear rings, grooved bushings, valve and pump parts, threaded sleeves, split bushings, and fully custom bronze components from drawings or operating data.

Plain Aluminum Bronze Sleeve Bushings

For shafts, pivots, linkages, rollers, hinges, fixtures, and sliding interfaces. We control bore, OD, length, roundness, wall thickness, finish, concentricity, and running clearance against the shaft and housing in C95400, C95500, C95800, C63000, or specified grades.

Flanged and Shoulder Aluminum Bronze Bushings

For axial location, load distribution, pull-through resistance, and controlled seating in machinery, valves, pumps, marine, and construction assemblies. We check flange and shoulder geometry, thrust-face flatness, bore transition, press fit, and replacement access; grooves, oil holes, slots, and mounting features can be added.

Grooved, Lubricated, and Oil-Hole Bushings

Axial, circumferential, spiral, cross-drilled, pocketed, and relief features support grease retention, oil distribution, debris relief, or fluid lubrication. Groove depth, edge radius, hole position, remaining wall, deburring, and alignment are checked to protect the bearing surface.

Thrust aluminum bronze bushing

For axial load, pump wear control, fluid equipment, marine hardware, and high-cycle machinery. We control thrust-face flatness, parallelism, ID/OD, grooves, hardness, finish, and edges, matching the material to the mating steel, lubrication, pressure, temperature, cavitation, and corrosion.

Threaded, Split, and Special-Feature Bronze Bushings

For valve bodies, marine assemblies, serviceable housings, retainers, and custom interfaces. Internal or external threads, blind bores, split lines, slots, flats, cross holes, retaining grooves, and seals are reviewed for thread strength, wall thickness, tool access, torque, burrs, and corrosion.

Aluminum Bronze Graphite Bushings

Start with a drawing, STEP file, shaft and housing dimensions, load and speed data, seawater exposure, lubrication notes, or annual usage. We support special grooves, thin walls, large diameters, complex flanges, oil holes, difficult grades, marking, coating, and service packaging, with a 24-hour quote and 48-hour DFM.

Supply options include material certificates, XRF verification, hardness, dimensional, bore, concentricity, and surface-finish records, C of C, PPAP Level 3, AS9102 FAIR, protective oil or VCI packaging, laser marking, and service-part labels.

Industries & Applications

For OEMs that need high wear resistance, strength, low friction, seawater compatibility, impact tolerance, and dependable replacement parts.

Marine

For rudders, propeller shafts, deck machinery, winches, pumps, seawater valves, hinges, and offshore equipment. C95400, C95500, and C95800 are reviewed for corrosion, load, impact, lubrication, and compatibility, with large sleeves, flanges, wear rings, thrust parts, and traceability supported.

Robotics

For robotics bushing. We review pressure, flow, chemistry, cavitation, temperature, shaft material, clearance, and finish, supporting C95800 cast-equivalent and machined C95400/C95500 programs.

Mining

Automotive

Sleeve bushings, guides, thrust washers, and threaded parts for cylinders, actuators, automation, fixtures, and motion systems. Alloy and clearance are matched to shaft hardness, speed, oil or grease, pressure, temperature, and duty cycle, with the same DFM for production and maintenance parts.

Aerospace

For actuator linkages, landing or braking mechanisms, vibration-resistant joints, vehicle systems, test equipment, and transportation hardware. PPAP Level 3, AS9102 FAIR, hardness records, traceability, first-article inspection, Cpk studies, and bar-coded packaging are available.

Energy

For turbines, generators, water systems, power equipment, process machinery, gears, and outdoor systems. C95400, C95500, C95800, and special alloys are matched to load, wear, temperature, vibration, water chemistry, and maintenance interval, with repeat-order and service packaging support.

Aluminum Bronze Bushing FAQ

Answers to common questions about alloy selection, clearance, wear, marine service, inspection, and lead time.

Send a 2D drawing or STEP file with alloy, hardness or condition, ID, OD, length, flange or shoulder dimensions, groove and oil-hole details, finish, quantity, and packaging notes. Without a drawing, shaft diameter and hardness, housing fit, load, speed, lubrication, temperature, environment, duty cycle, and annual usage are enough to start.

C95400 is a common starting point for general wear, marine, valve, pump, and industrial bushings. C95500 adds strength and load; C95800 suits marine, seawater, valve, and pump service; C63000 and C62300 support high-strength wrought programs. DFM confirms the best match for load, speed, shaft, lubrication, hardness, corrosion, temperature, and budget.

Clearance depends on shaft diameter, hardness and finish, bushing ID, load, sliding or oscillating speed, lubrication, temperature, contamination, and thermal expansion. Too little can cause seizure; too much can increase vibration, leakage, and wear. DFM confirms bore size, finish, concentricity, and fit for the actual operating condition.

Simple prototypes typically take 5–8 business days after drawing approval and material availability. Large diameters, C95800, special hardness, cast stock, grooved or flanged geometry, tight bores, and detailed documentation may take 8–15 business days. Volume timing depends on material, cycle time, inspection, packaging, and quantity. Quotes arrive within 24 hours and DFM within 48 hours for most projects.
