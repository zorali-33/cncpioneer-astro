Aluminum CNC Machining Flange

An aluminum CNC machining flange provides a bolted connection between a shaft, housing, pipe or equipment assembly. Turning and milling produce the locating diameter, central bore, mounting face, bolt pattern and any drawing-defined sealing features.

The machining plan follows the mating components and intended service. Define the alloy and temper, critical fits, face requirements and final surface treatment together so the flange can be inspected in its specified delivery condition.

Mounting & locating features

Define pilot diameters, bores, shoulders and mounting faces from the assembly datums.

Bolt patterns & threads

Specify hole positions, counterbores, thread details and fastener access on the drawing.

Sealing faces & grooves

Identify the seal arrangement, face finish, flatness and groove dimensions when sealing is required.

Finished-part requirements

Confirm coating allowances, masked surfaces, edge condition and the required inspection records.

Aluminum Alloys for Machined Flanges

6061-T6 / T651

A common option for machined mounting and adapter flanges. Confirm the stock form, delivery condition and final requirements for fits, faces and treatment.

6082-T6

An option for drawing-specified structural and equipment flanges. Review the required material standard, available stock section and surface finish with the quotation.

7075-T6 / T651

For designs specifying higher-strength aluminum. Confirm the material condition, service environment and finish compatibility rather than substituting alloys without drawing approval.

Surface Treatments for Aluminum Flanges

As-Machined & Deburred

Retain the machined surface with specified burr removal and edge breaks. Protect sealing faces and locating features during handling.

Bead Blasting

Prepare permitted surfaces for a uniform matte appearance. Define protected fits, threads and seal faces before blasting.

Type II Anodizing

Clear or colored anodizing to the agreed finish specification. Review dimensional allowance and contact areas before machining.

Type III Hard Anodizing

A hard anodic coating where specified by the design. Account for coating effects on bores, registers and threaded features.

Chemical Conversion Coating

Review conversion coating against the alloy and service requirements. State electrical contact, paint compatibility and masking needs on the drawing.

Electroless Nickel

A plated finish subject to alloy and process review. Agree preparation, coating allowance and inspection requirements for functional flange surfaces.

Aluminum Flange Machining FAQ

Which aluminum flange designs can you machine?

We review mounting flanges, adapters, flanged hubs, rings, robot interfaces and threaded configurations from customer drawings. The component envelope, wall thickness, features and material condition determine the machining route.

Which aluminum alloy should I specify?

6061, 6082 and 7075 can be reviewed in the specified temper and stock form. Selection must follow the design loads, operating environment, mating materials and surface treatment. Use the alloy required by the approved drawing; substitutions need customer approval.

What tolerances and sealing-face finish can you achieve?

Capability is reviewed for the flange size, alloy, geometry, fixturing and finishing route. Specify the critical bore and register fits, hole positions, face geometry, groove dimensions and roughness on the drawing. Define whether acceptance dimensions apply before or after coating.

How does anodizing affect flange fits and threads?

Anodizing changes the surface dimensions, with the effect depending on the process, alloy and preparation. Identify the final fit limits, coating thickness and masked areas before machining. Specify any protected seal faces, electrical contact areas or threaded features.

What is needed for aluminum flange machining quotes?

Send the 2D drawing and 3D model, aluminum grade and temper, quantity, required finish and delivery date. Mark the critical fits, face requirements and inspection documents. CNCPioneer in China will review the scope before confirming the quote and schedule.
