What Is an
Introducer Needle?

An introducer needle is a hollow-bore medical needle used to establish initial percutaneous access to a target anatomical space, vessel, or tissue plane — through which a guidewire, catheter, cannula, or secondary needle is subsequently advanced. The introducer needle serves as the first step in a sequential access procedure: the needle creates the initial tissue pathway, and the therapeutic or diagnostic device follows through or alongside it.

Introducer needles are fundamental to minimally invasive medicine. Without reliable introducer needle access, procedures including central venous catheterization, spinal anesthesia, epidural analgesia, lumbar puncture, interventional radiology catheter placement, and endoscopic intervention would require open surgical approaches with significantly greater patient risk and recovery burden. Types of Introducer Needles
We Manufacture

CNCPioneer's introducer needle factory manufactures the complete range of introducer needle types across all clinical specialties and gauge sizes — from the standard spinal introducer needle to specialty interventional radiology and coaxial biopsy configurations.

Spinal Introducer Needle

The most widely used introducer needle type globally — a short (~40mm), large-gauge (18G or 19G) needle that creates the tissue pathway and directional guide for the fine-gauge spinal needle, preventing skin-plug formation and deflection through tough fascial layers.

Pencil Point Spinal Needle with Introducer

The current clinical standard for spinal anesthesia — a matched pencil-point spinal needle and introducer set. Associated with significantly lower rates of post-dural puncture headache (PDPH). Whitacre and Sprotte designs supported.

Coaxial Introducer Needle

A larger-gauge outer needle through which a finer inner needle or catheter is advanced to the target tissue. The standard approach in CT-guided and ultrasound-guided biopsy — enabling multiple sampling passes through a single skin entry point.

Guidewire Introducer Needle

Designed for Seldinger technique vascular and non-vascular access — thin-wall construction maximizes inner lumen diameter for guidewire passage, with a short bevel tip optimized for vessel wall puncture rather than tissue dissection.

Central Line Introducer Needle

The access component of central venous catheter (CVC) insertion kits — reliable vessel puncture with blood flashback confirmation, smooth inner wall for atraumatic guidewire passage, and hub geometry compatible with CVC kit components.

Vascular, Epidural & IR Introducer Needles

Vascular introducer needles with echogenic tip treatment for ultrasound-guided access; anesthesia introducer needles for epidural and CSE techniques; and interventional radiology introducer needles across a wide range of lengths and gauges for image-guided procedures.

Introducer Needle FAQ

Common questions from medical device OEMs, introducer needle brands, and healthcare distributors about CNCPioneer's introducer needle manufacturing, OEM, custom, and wholesale programs.

An introducer needle is a hollow-bore needle designed specifically to establish a tissue access pathway through which a secondary device — spinal needle, guidewire, catheter, or biopsy needle — is subsequently advanced. Unlike a standard hypodermic needle used for direct drug injection, the introducer needle's primary function is access creation rather than fluid delivery. Introducer needles are typically shorter and stiffer than the needles they introduce, feature hub designs compatible with stylet insertion and removal, and have inner lumen dimensions precisely matched to the outer diameter of the device that passes through them.

The introducer needle 18G has an outer diameter of 1.27mm and inner diameter of approximately 0.84mm, making it compatible with standard 0.035" guidewires and 25G–27G spinal needles — the most widely used gauge for standard spinal anesthesia and central venous access applications. The introducer needle 20G has a smaller outer diameter of 0.90mm and inner diameter of approximately 0.58mm, used where a finer introducer is preferred for patient comfort, particularly in pediatric and ambulatory anesthesia settings. The 20G introducer needle is compatible with 25G–27G pencil-point spinal needles but does not accommodate standard 0.035" guidewires.

The spinal introducer needle is a short, large-gauge access needle that penetrates skin and subcutaneous tissue to create a stable tissue pathway for the finer diagnostic or anesthetic spinal needle that follows. The spinal needle itself — which may be a pencil-point Whitacre, Sprotte, or cutting-bevel Quincke design — is advanced through the tissue tract created by the spinal introducer needle to reach the subarachnoid space. The spinal introducer needle prevents skin-plug formation on the spinal needle tip and provides directional guidance through tough fascial layers. The two needles work as a system — the spinal introducer needle market is therefore closely linked to the market for fine-gauge pencil-point spinal needles.
