Why Swiss Screw Machining Is Ideal for Connector Pins

Connector pins are often small, slender, and tolerance-sensitive, making them an excellent fit for Swiss screw machining. The Swiss process provides enhanced stability during machining, especially valuable for long, thin, or miniature components.

Swiss machining is widely used for miniature and precision cylindrical parts because it supports intricate features, tight tolerances, and repeatable output at production scale.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are connector pins made from? +
Connector pins are commonly made from brass, copper alloys, stainless steel, and other specialty metals depending on conductivity, corrosion resistance, and mechanical requirements.
Why use Swiss screw machining for connector pins? +
Swiss machining is ideal for connector pins because it provides better support for small-diameter, slender, and tight-tolerance parts, helping achieve consistent precision and surface finish.
Can you manufacture miniature connector pins? +
Yes. Swiss machining is especially suitable for miniature and micro-scale connector pin geometries used in electronics, medical, and precision devices.
Do you offer plating services? +
Yes. Depending on the application, plating options may include nickel, tin, gold, silver, or other specified finishes.
Can you make custom connector pins from drawings? +
Yes. We manufacture custom connector pins based on customer drawings, dimensions, material requirements, and performance specifications.
What industries use machined connector pins? +
Common industries include electronics, medical, industrial equipment, telecommunications, aerospace, and defense.
